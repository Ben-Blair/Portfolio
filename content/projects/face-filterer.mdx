A desktop tool for the specific misery of having four thousand photos from an event and needing the
two hundred with one particular person in them.

You drag a folder onto the window, give it a face to look for, and it works through the pile with
`face_recognition` over OpenCV, previewing matches in real time and zipping up the results at the
end. Progress tracking mattered more than I expected — a face-recognition pass over thousands of
full-resolution images takes long enough that a frozen window reads as a crash.

Built with Tkinter and `tkinterdnd2` for drag-and-drop, Pillow for the previews. Not glamorous, but
it's the project of mine that non-programmers have actually asked to use.
